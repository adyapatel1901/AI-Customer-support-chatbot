TECHSTORE CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

COMPANY INFORMATION

TechStore is an online electronics store that sells
laptops, smartphones, headphones, accessories and other
electronic products.


ORDER INFORMATION

Customers can track their orders using their order ID.

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days.

Customers receive an order confirmation after successfully
placing an order.


CANCELLATION POLICY

An order can be cancelled before it is shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may not be
available.


RETURN POLICY

Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.

Products should be returned in acceptable condition.

Customers should contact customer support if they want
to initiate a return.


REFUND POLICY

Refunds are processed after the returned product has been
received and inspected.

Refund processing generally takes 5 to 7 business days.


PAYMENT METHODS

TechStore supports:

- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- UPI
- Net Banking


PAYMENT PROBLEMS

If money has been deducted but the order was not created,
the customer should contact customer support.

The support team may create a support ticket to investigate
the payment issue.


ACCOUNT SUPPORT

Customers can contact support for:

- Login problems
- Password problems
- Account problems
- Profile issues


CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customer support is available Monday to Saturday,
9 AM to 6 PM.

Customers can request human-agent assistance when the
AI assistant cannot resolve their issue.


IMPORTANT RULE

The AI assistant must not invent company policies,
prices, order statuses, refund amounts or delivery dates.

If the required information is not available, the AI
assistant should ask the customer to contact human support.
